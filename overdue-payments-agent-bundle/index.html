FAQ

What is an overdue invoice tracker?

It is a system for recording unpaid invoices, due dates, reminder stages, last follow-up dates, next follow-up dates, and payment status.

Does this send payment reminders automatically?

No. It is designed to draft and organize follow-ups for review, so you stay in control before sending.

Who is this for?

Freelancers, agencies, consultants, contractors, and small business owners who need a cleaner way to manage overdue payments.

Is this accounting software?

No. It is a lightweight tracking and follow-up system for unpaid invoices and payment reminders.

Can I customize the reminder stages?

Yes. You can adjust the stages, notes, client fields, and follow-up rhythm.
