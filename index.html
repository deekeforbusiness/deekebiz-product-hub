Common questions

Are these official Notion products?

No. DeekeBiz is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Notion.

Do the guides require buying a template?

No. Each guide is designed to be useful on its own. Product links are included when a ready-made structure can save implementation time.

Can the templates be customized?

Yes. The systems are intended to be duplicated and adapted to your fields, stages, views, responsibilities, and review rhythm.
