The DeekeBiz standard

Process first. Bounded AI second.

A fragile workflow hides behind a polished dashboard or a long prompt. A usable workflow makes the trigger, owner, inputs, decisions, exceptions, review rules, output, and maintenance responsibility visible. AI is added only where the task and acceptance criteria are clear enough to control.

What this avoids

  • Automating undocumented work
  • Letting AI make authority-sensitive decisions
  • Testing only ideal inputs
  • Launching without a fallback

What this creates

  • Repeatable SOPs and handoffs
  • Human approval at the right points
  • Visible exceptions and audit records
  • A pilot that can be paused safely

Common questions

Is DeekeBiz an AI automation agency?

No. DeekeBiz creates self-serve guides, diagnostics, and Notion systems for designing and documenting operational workflows. It does not deploy software into your business.

Do I need Notion to use the free resources?

No. The readiness scorecard and workflow guides are useful independently. Notion is the implementation layer used in the paid systems.

Should every workflow use AI?

No. Some workflows need clearer ownership, a checklist, or deterministic automation rather than AI. The recommended design is the least complex system that reliably produces the required outcome.
