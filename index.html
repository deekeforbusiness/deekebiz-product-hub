Before you choose

A few clear answers.

Every product page shows its price, format, and requirements.

Do I need Notion?

The Notion workspaces require your own Notion account. PDF guides and the free browser tools can be used without Notion. Check each product’s requirements before buying.

Is this a subscription?

DeekeBiz products are one-time purchases. Any separate software plan or usage cost is listed in the relevant product’s requirements.

What does the “+” after a price mean?

That is the minimum price. Gumroad lets you pay more if you choose.

Can I try something before buying?

Yes. Every product page has three actual workspace or PDF previews. The ten ClarityPack10 browser tools are also free to use without an account.
