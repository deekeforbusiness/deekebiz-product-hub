Before you buy

Requires your own Notion account and property information. Uses fixed-rate amortizing financing or a cash purchase. DSCR is blank without debt service; cash-on-cash return is blank when no cash is invested. Missing required or invalid inputs leave calculations blank. Fixed screening thresholds are explained in the guide. Appreciation, sale proceeds and taxes on investment returns are outside this model.
