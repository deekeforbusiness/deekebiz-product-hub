Questions before you begin

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. No account or payment is required. The Pro Bundle is an optional deeper set of printable workbooks and editable scripts.

Where does my information go?

Your entries remain in this browser and may be saved locally on this device. They are not submitted to DeekeBiz. Do not use a shared device for sensitive work without clearing the draft.

Will a report make the decision for me?

No. The tools organize information and questions. They do not provide legal, medical, financial, insurance, contractor or other professional advice.
