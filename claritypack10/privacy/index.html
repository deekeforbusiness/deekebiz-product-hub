Privacy

Your entries stay on your device.

The ClarityPack10 tools run in your browser. They do not send the information you type to DeekeBiz.

What the tools save

To restore a draft after a refresh, a tool may store your entries in that browser’s local storage. Local storage is controlled by your browser and device. Selecting Clear removes the saved draft for that tool from that browser.

What the tools do not request

Do not enter passwords, PINs, recovery codes, complete payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers or other secrets that are unnecessary for the report. Keep original evidence in a secure location appropriate for the information.

Printing and downloads

When you print or save a PDF, your browser and operating system handle the file. You are responsible for choosing a secure location and limiting access to exported reports.

Shared devices

Clear the tool after use and remove downloaded or printed copies when working on a shared or public device. Private-browsing modes may handle local storage differently.

Links and report attribution

Reports include a public link to the tool. The link never contains your answers. Optional Gumroad product links include page and campaign labels, never report contents. Visiting Gumroad takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Scope

These tools are organizational and educational. They do not provide legal, medical, financial, insurance, contractor or other professional advice.
