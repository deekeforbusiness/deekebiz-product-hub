Before you buy

Requires your own Notion account and basic database editing. Related Deal links organize records; transfer selected assumptions and budgets into the main model manually. Stress Input Score is a heuristic, not a probability of loss. Rental revenue comparisons omit several costs. Sale-only annualized return excludes interim rental cash flow and taxes. Refinance cash-out is borrowed money; the rate field does not calculate a new loan payment. Verify assumptions and read Model Definitions & Limits.
